# Church Web App Design Preferences

## Design Theme
The overall design should feel:
- Elegant
- Spiritual
- Modern
- Welcoming
- Professional
- Minimal yet visually impactful

The website should represent the warmth and dignity of the church while maintaining a clean and modern interface.

---

# Landing Page Preferences

## Hero Section
The landing page should contain:
- Fullscreen background image
- Lead pastor and family image
- Dark blue overlay for readability
- Welcome text
- Church slogan or Bible verse
- Gold-accented CTA button

### Hero Style
- Elegant fade-in animation
- Subtle zoom/parallax effect
- Soft shadow overlays
- Responsive scaling
